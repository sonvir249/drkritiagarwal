Patient Guidance & Respiratory Triage

When Should You Consult a Pulmonologist?

Early diagnosis of airway conditions prevents permanent lung scarring. If you are experiencing any of these common chest symptoms in Mumbai, Navi Mumbai, Kharghar, or Panvel, schedule a diagnostic checkup:

Persistent cough lasting more than 2–3 weeks?

Breathlessness or shortness of breath while walking or climbing stairs?

Recurrent wheezing, whistling breath sounds, or chest tightness?

Frequent respiratory infections, seasonal bronchitis, or recurring pneumonia?

Blood in sputum or phlegm (Hemoptysis)?

Cough that worsens at night, early morning, or during weather shifts?

Chronic nasal allergy, dust sensitivity, or breathing difficulty?

Poor asthma symptom control despite regular daily inhaler use?

Persistent breathlessness or post-infectious lung damage after TB treatment?

Heavy loud snoring, choking in sleep, or severe daytime drowsiness?

Unexplained low oxygen saturation levels (SpO2 drops below 94%)?

Active smoking or tobacco history with chronic smoker's cough or phlegm?

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding chest illnesses, EBUS bronchoscopy, and pulmonary consultations in Mumbai.

When should I see a pulmonologist instead of a general physician?

You should consult a pulmonologist (chest specialist) if you experience a persistent cough lasting more than 2–3 weeks, unexplained breathlessness, recurrent wheezing, blood in sputum, chest pain on breathing, or asthma symptoms that do not improve with basic inhalers.

What is EBUS-TBNA and is it available in Mumbai and Navi Mumbai?

Endobronchial Ultrasound (EBUS) is a safe, minimally invasive procedure using a specialized bronchoscope equipped with ultrasound to accurately sample enlarged lymph nodes and lung nodules in the chest without requiring open surgery.

About Dr. Kriti Agarwal

Comprehensive respiratory medicine combining evidence-based academic medicine with minimally invasive interventional technologies.

Dr. Kriti Agarwal is a specialist Respiratory Physician and Interventional Pulmonologist based in Mumbai[cite: 4, 5]. Having completed postgraduate residency at Indira Gandhi Government Medical College and senior clinical tenures at premier Mumbai medical colleges (TNMC & B.Y.L. Nair Charitable Hospital and Grant Government Medical College & Sir J.J. Group of Hospitals), she brings extensive experience in diagnosing and triaging complicated chest conditions[cite: 5, 6].

She holds the prestigious European Diploma in Adult Respiratory Medicine (EDARM - HERMES) conferred by the European Respiratory Society and is a Diplomate of the National Board (DNB)[cite: 5, 6]. Her sub-speciality focus centers on advanced diagnostic bronchoscopy, pleural diseases, allergy immunotherapy, and ICU respiratory failure[cite: 5].

Clinical Practice Standards

  • Precise Early Diagnosis: Utilization of Linear/Radial EBUS, cryobiopsy, and high-resolution imaging for undiagnosed lung nodules and mediastinal masses[cite: 5].
  • Patient-Centric Counseling: Compassionate explanation of chronic disease trajectories including Asthma, COPD, ILD, and Tuberculosis to patients and families.

Areas of Practice

Procedural & Clinical Competence

Four specialized procedural domains delivered with tertiary-care precision.

Interventional Pulmonology

Linear EBUS-TBNA for mediastinal staging, Radial EBUS for peripheral pulmonary nodules, flexible bronchoscopy (BAL, TBLB, endobronchial biopsy), spigot placement, and rigid bronchoscopy tumor debulking[cite: 5].

Pleural Interventions

Ultrasound-guided diagnostic and therapeutic thoracocentesis, small-bore pigtail catheter insertions, trocar tube thoracostomy (ICD), medical thoracoscopy, and bedside chemical pleurodesis[cite: 5].

Critical Care & Access

Emergency endotracheal intubations, percutaneous dilatational tracheostomy, central venous catheterization (USG-guided IJV, subclavian, femoral), hemodialysis double-lumen lines, and mechanical ventilation[cite: 5].

Physiology, Sleep & Allergy

Comprehensive pulmonary function testing (Spirometry, DLCO, Plethysmography, FeNO, Oscillometry), Level 1 Polysomnography & CPAP titration, Skin Prick Testing, and biological therapy[cite: 5].
